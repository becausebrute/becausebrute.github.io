John Brute

I’m a Welsh artist who now resides just outside of Asheville, NC. Within this portfolio is the artwork that I create, as well as tattoo ideas (both digitally and hand-drawn).

As a 40-something undergoing a career transition from an engineer into the tattoo industry, I understand the necessity of patience, tenacity, and lifelong learning. Each piece displayed is one more stride toward my quest for an apprenticeship.

Thank you for checking out my work.
